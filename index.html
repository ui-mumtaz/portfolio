About

Twelve years of Angular, from
NgModules to Signals.

I’ve spent twelve years watching Angular reinvent itself — and shipped through every version of it. 150+ projects later, I still sweat the last 5% of polish as much as the first line of code.

These days that means the framework’s newest chapter: Standalone Components, Signals-based reactivity, Zoneless Change Detection and the new Control Flow & Deferrable Views, layered on RxJS, NgRx and Nx monorepos. I’ve built component libraries other teams adopted without being asked to, led framework upgrades that outlived the people who requested them, and kept real-time systems (WebSockets/STOMP) standing under load across retail, gaming, telecom and trading.

None of that lives in isolation from the rest of the stack. I’m just as at home in React (Hooks, Context, Redux Toolkit, React Query) or writing the Node.js / Express APIs underneath, against PostgreSQL or MongoDB, deployed on AWS. Somewhere along the way I became the person other engineers hand their code reviews to — and I’ve come to enjoy that as much as writing the code myself.
